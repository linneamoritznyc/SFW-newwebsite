A foundation built on living soil

For four decades, Dr. Elaine Ingham showed the world what farmers and foresters have always sensed: soil is not dirt. It is a living web of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, nematodes, and micro-arthropods that feeds plants, stores carbon, holds water, and sustains every terrestrial ecosystem on Earth.

The Soil Food Web Foundation was created in 2025 to carry that work forward. We teach the science through the Soil Food Web School, pursue and share new knowledge, incubate regenerative practice in ecoregions around the world, and build the community that makes it all last. The School is a program of the Foundation; course fees fund the charitable work.

Our mission and story

Teaching

Education in soil biology, from first curiosity to earned professional titles, with workshops and scholarships in a growing list of ecoregions.

Research

Replicating key experiments, opening our trial and case-study data, and advancing microscopy and lab methods.

Practice

Partnerships with farmers to trial and document the approach where they live, and ecoregion hubs that put compost, microscopy, consulting and education under one roof.

Community

Students, graduates, volunteers, donors and advisors in more than 100 countries who make this work outlast any one of us.
