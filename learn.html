Before you ask

  • Do I need a science background?

    No. The courses are designed for people with no relevant experience. The Foundation Courses build everything from first principles, at your pace.

  • How long do the programs take?

    They’re self-paced.

    VERIFY typical completion ranges per program with the education team.

  • What equipment do I need?

    The Foundation Courses need only a computer and curiosity. The Microscopy program requires a microscope. Guidance on affordable models is provided inside the course.

  • What do the professional titles mean?

    Soil Food Web Consultant: completed the full Complete Practicum (all four Advanced Programs). Soil Food Web Lab-Tech: completed the Microscopy Advanced Program. Both titles are earned, and both appear in our professionals directory.

  • What if it’s not for me?

    Every course carries a 30-day, no-questions-asked money-back guarantee, provided you’ve watched no more than one-third of the lectures.

  • I can’t afford the courses. Are there options?

    Yes. Our scholarship program is growing, with priority for applicants in under-resourced regions. See the Scholarships page.
