The two titles

Soil Food Web Consultant: completed the Complete Practicum. Soil Food Web Lab Technician: completed Soil Microscopy. Both are issued by the Soil Food Web Foundation on completion of assessed work. Neither is accredited by an external body. Both confer a listing in the practitioner directory.

Questions people ask

  • Do I need a science background?

    No. The Foundation Courses build from first principles.

  • How long does it take?

    Foundation Courses: about 150 hours, four weeks full time or spread across the 12-month access window. Complete Practicum: 18 to 24 months is typical; you have 42.

  • What equipment do I need?

    A computer for the Foundation Courses. A microscope and camera, about $1,000, for Soil Microscopy and the Complete Practicum. Compost Production can be started without one.

  • Where do the courses run?

    On school.soilfoodweb.com. You never need a second login for the courses above.

  • Can I get help paying?

    Scholarships prioritize applicants in under-resourced regions. See Scholarships.
