The ground beneath our farms and forests is alive.

A web of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, nematodes and micro-arthropods feeds plants, builds soil, stores carbon and cleans water when it is intact.

The Soil Food Web Foundation teaches people to see that web, restore it, and publish what happens when they do. It is a nonprofit and a school working together: education, open research, applied practice, and a global community, all pointed at the same problem.

Our story

It began with one scientist looking closer.

Dr. Elaine Ingham pioneered research in soil biology across four decades and made her science radically accessible, so that anyone, anywhere, could learn to restore the soil beneath their feet.

The Soil Food Web Foundation was created in 2025 to carry that work forward. Dr. Ingham died in February 2026. The work continues through the Foundation and the school inside it, teaching the science she mapped to anyone willing to learn it. Today students and practitioners in more than 100 countries carry the same close attention into their own soil.

Her published record, still to settle. This Foundation's own page for Dr. Ingham says 89 publications, 53 of them journal articles. The copy deck says 82 verified links and separately lists 77 journal papers. One figure is needed before print. Linnea and Evan.

Teaching

Anyone, anywhere, can learn to restore living soil.

From a first curiosity through to professional practice. Everyone starts with the Foundation Courses. Where you go next depends on whether you want to grow better yourself, or help other people do it.

Foundation Courses

Four courses covering the science of the soil food web, the organisms and their roles, and the fundamentals of making and applying biological amendments. Online, self-paced, about 150 hours, and required before any Advanced Program.

Advanced Programs and the Complete Practicum

Soil Microscopy, BioComplete™ Compost Production, Biological Liquid Amendments and Field Trial, each with a mentor for the duration of your studies. All four together are the Complete Practicum.

Accelerator Workshops

The Advanced Programs worked through as a group on working land, held periodically. The fastest way to see the whole method applied at once. The next runs 19 to 30 October 2026 at Save Soil Farm near the Isha Yoga Center, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu.
