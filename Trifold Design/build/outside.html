Teaching

Anyone, anywhere, can learn to restore living soil.

From first curiosity to professional practice. Everyone starts with the Foundation Courses. Where you go next depends on whether you want to grow better yourself, or help others do it.

Foundation Courses

The science of the soil food web, the organisms and their roles, and how biological amendments are made and applied. Online, self-paced, about 150 hours, and required before any Advanced Program.

Advanced Programs

Soil Microscopy, BioComplete™ Compost Production, Biological Liquid Amendments and Field Trial, each with its own mentor. All four together are the full path.

Accelerator Workshops

The Advanced Programs worked through as a group on working land, the whole method applied at once. Held periodically, and hosted with a partner on their land. Dates and places are listed with the programs.

Where it leads

All four Advanced Programs together carry the Soil Food Web Consultant title; Soil Microscopy alone, the Lab Technician title. Every program, with its price, is listed at school.soilfoodweb.com

The ground beneath our farms and forests is alive.

A web of bacteria, fungi, protozoa, nematodes and micro-arthropods feeds plants, builds soil, stores carbon and cleans water when it is intact.

For forty-five years, Dr. Elaine Ingham mapped that web and taught the world to restore it. We carry her work forward: teaching the science to anyone willing to learn, opening the research for the world to test, proving the practice on real land, and growing a global community of Soil Regenerators. Living soil is the solution, and it is beneath your feet.
