Enrollment records, confirmed August 2026.

What we test before we recommend

  • Measurable. Every recommendation starts with a microscope count, not a guess.
  • Diagnostic before prescriptive. We look at the biology already living in your soil before we suggest anything.
  • Compatible with the farm you have. No need to tear up an existing operation to start.

On claimsEach mechanism we teach is established soil biology. How much of any one benefit a field sees depends on climate, crop, starting condition and management. We publish results by site, with the person and the year, and say plainly what a single site cannot prove.

Pillar three of four · Practice

It works on real land, in the hands of real growers.

The biology is universal; the practice is local. Every number we publish is attached to a named grower, a named place and a year.

Eleven practitioners have filmed their own work and published it: Nick Padwick in Norfolk, England; Todd Harrington on corn and soy in Illinois; Roberto Silva in Piura, Peru; Renald Flores on a market garden in Sweden; and others in California, Ecuador, India and Mexico.

Pillar four of four · Community

A global community makes this work outlast any one of us.

Dr. Elaine gave this movement its science. The community gives it staying power. Soil Regenerators in more than 100 countries, on six continents, teach their neighbors, run their own trials, and restore land farm by farm.
