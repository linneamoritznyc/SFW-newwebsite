# Print specification

**Soil Food Web Foundation — 2026 event brochure, six-panel tri-fold**

Everything below is measured from the supplied file, not estimated. Anything the
shop needs to decide is marked **[confirm]**.

---

## 1. The file

**`sfw-trifold-2page-CMYK-PDFX.pdf`** — one file, two pages. Page 1 is the
outside of the sheet, page 2 is the inside. Print 4/4, work and turn on a single
sheet.

| | |
| :-- | :-- |
| Format | PDF 1.6, **PDF/X-4** |
| Pages | 2 |
| Page (media) size | 810 × 630 pt = **11.25 × 8.75 in** |
| TrimBox | 9 9 801 621 pt = **11 × 8.5 in**, centered |
| BleedBox / MediaBox | 0 0 810 630 pt (**0.125 in bleed all four sides**) |
| Color | CMYK throughout. No RGB, no spot colors, no overprint set |
| Fonts | 30, all embedded and subset |
| Transparency | Live (X-4). Not flattened |

A **PDF/X-1a** version is available if your RIP requires it:
`sfw-trifold-2page-CMYK-PDFX1a.pdf`. Please use the X-4 file if you can — see §8.

There are no printer's marks drawn on the artwork. The TrimBox carries the trim;
impose from the boxes. If you need visible crop marks and a slug, say so and we
will supply a version with them.

## 3. Imposition — please read before re-imposing

**The two pages are deliberately mirrored, and the file is correct as supplied.**
Because the tuck panel is a single physical flap, it falls on the right of one
side of the sheet and on the **left** of the other. Page 1's folds are therefore
at 3.625 / 7.3125 and page 2's at 3.6875 / 7.375, and each is the other
subtracted from 11.

If your prepress normalizes both pages to the same panel order, every panel ends
up 1/16 in out of register with its own back. Please impose as supplied.

Correctly folded, the panel reading "The ground beneath our farms and forests is
alive" is the **front cover** and ends face up.

## 4. Stock **[confirm]**

Recommended, in order of preference:

1. **100 lb text, silk or matte coated** — our first choice. Folds cleanly at
   this size, holds the photography, does not gloss-glare under event lighting.
2. **80 lb text, silk or matte coated** — lighter and cheaper; acceptable.
3. **80 lb cover / 9 pt C2S** — only if a stiffer piece is wanted. **Must be
   scored**, see §6.

Please advise what you stock and what runs best on your press.

- **Coating:** none required. If you recommend a coating for scuff resistance at
  events, **matte or satin aqueous** only. **No gloss UV** — it makes the QR
  codes harder to scan under direct light.
- **Opacity:** the inside has large solid dark greens backing white panels.
  Please use a stock with enough opacity to avoid show-through.

---

## 5. Ink and color

- **4/4 process CMYK.** No spot colors, no metallic, no varnish plate.
- **Output intent in the file is a generic CMYK profile.** Please **re-target to
  your own press condition** — GRACoL 2013 (CRPC6) for coated sheetfed, or SWOP
  2013 (CRPC5) for web, or whatever you characterize to. We would rather you
  convert to a profile you have measured than trust ours.
- **Total area coverage, measured from the file: 302% on page 1, 311% on page 2**
  (99.9th percentile 288% / 290%).
  - Fine for **coated sheetfed**.
  - If you are running **uncoated or web**, this exceeds a typical 240–260%
    limit. Tell us your number and we will re-separate to it rather than have
    you guess. **[confirm]**
- **Rich blacks:** the dark greens are built in process, not as rich black
  overprints. Nothing needs trapping by hand.

---

## 6. Finishing

- **Score before folding.** Required on 80 lb cover; recommended on 100 lb text,
  because both folds cross large solid dark areas and unscored folds will crack.
- **Grain direction parallel to the folds** (grain long on an 11 × 8.5 sheet).
- Fold to the panel widths in §2, not to equal thirds.
- **[confirm]** Quantity, packing, and whether you want them banded in counts.

---

## 7. Proofing — one specific request

Please supply a **hard-copy contract proof** on the actual stock, not a screen
PDF proof.

**When the proof comes off the press, scan all five QR codes with a phone.** They
are printed at 29.2 mm square, 0.649 mm per module, error correction H. The gold
code on the back panel is the lightest ink of the five and has the least margin —
**check that one first.** If any code fails to scan on press output, stop and
contact us; we have darker versions of the two lightest colors ready and it is a
five-minute change on our side.

---

## 8. Two things preflight will flag, and why they are correct

**1. A handful of low-resolution images per page, the largest around 45 ppi.**
These are the smooth two-color gradients behind the panel headers. They are
color ramps with no detail, not photographs, and they are tagged `/Interpolate`
so the RIP smooths them. They are not an error and do not need replacing.
All photographs in the file are **400–800 ppi** and nothing is downsampled.

**2. Type 3 fonts.** All 30 fonts are fully embedded and subset. Some are Type 3
because the brand's headline face is a variable font. They render and separate
correctly.

**Why we prefer the X-4 file:** PDF/X-1a forbids live transparency, so producing
it requires flattening, and flattening this design rasterizes the whole sheet.
Our X-1a file is flattened at 600 dpi and every QR code was verified to still
decode from it, so it is safe to use — but the X-4 file keeps the type and all
five QR codes as live vector artwork, which will image more crisply. Use X-4
unless your workflow cannot.
